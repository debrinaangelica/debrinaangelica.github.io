Problem set 7 for 24672.

For this project, we had to create a product that makes myself or someone else happy. It should be something I would enjoy using myself or give to someone else as a gift. For this project, I decided to create a fruit bowl. The idea originated because I have a friend who leaves his fruits by the kitchen sink, and over time, the fruits usually end up living in a puddle of water. I thought a fruit bowl would be a good way to solve this problem.
